THE THERMAL DIVIDE - how to open

1. Unzip ThermalDivide.zip (right-click > Extract All on Windows; double-click on Mac).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

Everything (app, data and heat maps) is inside that one index.html file, so you can
move or email it on its own. Needs an internet connection only for the grey background
street map. No install, no login.
Tip: click "Case file" (top left) for a guided tour.
